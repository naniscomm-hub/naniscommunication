THE STORY

More Than a Performer

Park Jimin was born on 13 October 1995 in Busan, South Korea. Long before international stadiums, award ceremonies and fashion headlines became part of his life, he was a young student drawn to movement and performance. His early interest in dance developed into serious training, and he studied contemporary dance at Busan High School of Arts. That foundation would later become one of the defining elements of his stage identity.

Jimin's path was not an overnight success story. He auditioned for Big Hit Entertainment and moved to Seoul to train. The transition demanded persistence: a new environment, demanding schedules, vocal development and the pressure of preparing for a debut. He became a trainee in 2012 and made his official debut with BTS on 13 June 2013.

Within the seven-member group, Jimin developed a role that combines singing with movement. His performances often balance precision and fluidity. A turn, a pause, a hand gesture or a shift in expression can carry as much meaning as a vocal phrase. This quality makes him recognisable not simply as a dancer who sings, but as a performer who treats the stage as a space for storytelling.

THE ARTIST

A Voice That Carries Emotion

A distinctive vocal colour

Jimin's voice is often recognised for its clear upper register, delicate texture and expressive phrasing. Rather than relying only on power, he frequently shapes a line through breath, tone and emotional detail. In BTS songs, his vocal parts can add a sense of vulnerability, urgency or warmth, depending on the mood of the composition.

His artistry is especially evident in songs that require emotional contrast. He can move from a soft, intimate delivery to a more intense chorus while maintaining a recognisable vocal character. Like every vocalist, his technique continues to evolve through experience, practice and live performance.

The language of dance

Jimin's contemporary dance background is visible in the way he uses space and movement. His lines are often fluid, his transitions carefully controlled and his gestures expressive. He can make choreography appear effortless while maintaining the strength and stamina required for demanding group performances.

In songs such as “Black Swan”, the connection between music and movement becomes particularly clear. The performance explores the fear of losing passion for one's art, and Jimin's movement helps translate that emotional idea into a visual experience.

“A performance is not only about what the audience sees. It is also about what they feel.”
THE BTS YEARS

Growing Together with Six Members

Jimin debuted as one of the seven members of BTS alongside RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, V and Jung Kook. The group began in 2013 and gradually developed a musical identity that crossed genres and languages. Their work explored youth, ambition, uncertainty, self-worth, social pressure, love and the experience of growing up.

As BTS expanded internationally, Jimin became a familiar presence in music videos, live stages, award-show performances and global tours. He contributed to the group's vocal arrangements and participated in the emotional storytelling that helped define many of its songs. His solo moments within group albums also gave listeners a closer view of his personal artistic interests.

Among the songs and performances associated with Jimin are “Lie”, “Serendipity”, “Filter”, “Friends” with V, “Black Swan”, “The Truth Untold” and “Spring Day”. These tracks show different sides of his musical personality: dramatic, playful, tender, introspective and powerful.

His relationship with the other members is also part of the BTS story. The group's long journey involved years of training, touring, recording and living through major changes together. For ARMY, the friendship and creative chemistry among the seven members are as meaningful as the music itself.

SOLO MILESTONES

FACE: Finding His Own Voice

Jimin's solo career gave him room to explore personal themes with a different level of focus. On 24 March 2023, he released his first solo album, FACE. The project includes “Face-off”, “Interlude: Dive”, “Like Crazy”, “Alone” and “Set Me Free Pt.2”, along with the English version of “Like Crazy”.

The album was introduced as a reflection on facing himself and moving through emotions that had built up during a period of uncertainty. Its sound moves between pop, hip-hop, R&B and synth-pop. “Like Crazy”, the title track, is driven by a synth-pop arrangement that contrasts with the wistful quality of Jimin's voice.

“Like Crazy” made history by debuting at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated 8 April 2023. Jimin became the first South Korean solo artist to top the chart. The achievement was a major milestone, but it also represented the growing international reach of Korean-language and Korean-led pop music.

“Set Me Free Pt.2” presented a different energy: bold, theatrical and determined. Together, the tracks showed that Jimin's solo identity was not limited to one mood or genre. He was willing to experiment, to take creative risks and to let the music reflect different parts of his experience.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

MUSE: Exploring Love and Inspiration

On 19 July 2024, Jimin released his second solo album, MUSE. The seven-track project follows his exploration of inspiration, affection and the search for a meaningful connection. It includes “Rebirth (Intro)”, “Interlude: Showtime”, “Smeraldo Garden Marching Band” featuring Loco, “Slow Dance” featuring Sofia Carson, “Be Mine”, “Who” and “Closer Than This”.

“Who” became the album's central track and drew wide international attention. Its sound combines contemporary pop and R&B elements, while the lyrics explore longing for someone who feels close in imagination but remains unknown in reality. The track's choreography and performance style gave Jimin another opportunity to connect movement with emotional storytelling.

MUSE followed the success of FACE while showing artistic continuity. Where FACE looked inward at a period of personal reflection, MUSE opened a wider conversation about inspiration and the search for connection. The two albums form an important part of Jimin's development as a solo artist.

FASHION & PERSONAL STYLE

An Artist with a Distinctive Visual Identity

Jimin's influence extends beyond music. His appearance at international fashion events has brought him into conversations about contemporary menswear, luxury design and the relationship between performance and personal style. His work with Dior as a global ambassador placed him within a fashion house known for its history, craftsmanship and evolving creative direction.

One reason his style attracts attention is its versatility. He can move from a soft, romantic image to a sharply tailored silhouette, from relaxed streetwear to stage costumes built around movement and drama. His clothing choices often work with the way he performs: fabrics, proportions and details become part of the overall visual story.

In 2026, his longer hair added another dimension to this image. The look appeared in different forms across public appearances and tour stages, including flowing blonde lengths and more textured styling. Vogue's coverage of his Dior preparation described his decision to grow his hair and pursue a more refined image. This was not simply a hairstyle change; it became part of the visual evolution of an artist entering a new phase of his career.

Fashion is only one part of Jimin's identity, however. It complements the music rather than replacing it. His most lasting connection with audiences continues to come from the voice, movement and emotional commitment he brings to performance.

THE ARMY CONNECTION

Warmth Beyond the Spotlight

Jimin's relationship with ARMY is a recurring theme in his public life. Through concerts, interviews and messages on fan platforms, he has often expressed gratitude for the people who support BTS and his solo work. For many fans, the connection is built not only through major achievements but also through small gestures: a message after a show, a moment of humour, a personal photograph or a sincere expression of thanks.

His stage persona can be intense and charismatic, yet fans also see a softer, playful and affectionate side in behind-the-scenes content. This contrast is part of what makes his public image compelling. The performer who commands a stadium can also appear thoughtful and approachable in everyday moments.

It is important to remember that artists are also people entitled to privacy and rest. Supporting Jimin means celebrating his work while respecting his boundaries, personal time and well-being. A healthy fan culture allows admiration to remain positive and sustainable.

MILITARY SERVICE & RETURN

A Pause, a Responsibility, a New Beginning

Jimin enlisted for South Korea's mandatory military service in December 2023 alongside Jung Kook. The two served in the Army's 5th Infantry Division and were discharged on 11 June 2025 after completing their service. Their return was an important moment for fans who had followed the members through the group's period of individual military duties.

Military service marked a pause in public group activities, but it did not erase the work that had come before. Jimin returned with new experiences and a different stage of life behind him. For an artist whose career began at a young age, this transition represented another chapter in personal growth.

The completion of military service by all seven members made a full-group return possible. It also gave BTS and ARMY a shared point of anticipation: the chance to see the members together again, with each bringing the experiences and artistic development of the intervening years.

THE ARIRANG ERA

Jimin Returns to the Global Stage with BTS

2026 marked a new chapter for BTS as the seven members returned to group activity in the ARIRANG era. The comeback connected the group's contemporary sound with themes of Korean identity, memory and cultural expression. For Jimin, returning to the stage with RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, V and Jung Kook meant rejoining a creative partnership that had shaped much of his adult life.

The ARIRANG world tour also gave fans around the world the opportunity to experience the group together again. Jimin's long blonde hair, stage styling and expressive performance became recurring talking points, but the central attraction remained the music and the energy of seven performers sharing one stage.

By November 2026, BTS had continued touring across international markets, with Southeast Asian dates including Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Bangkok and Jakarta forming part of the wider regional schedule. The tour brought the group closer to fans across the region and renewed the sense of connection that has always been central to BTS's relationship with ARMY.

For Malaysian fans, the prospect of seeing BTS in the region adds a special local dimension to the ARIRANG story. It is a reminder that a group formed in Seoul can create a shared cultural moment across countries, languages and generations.
