THE STORY

An Artist with Many Sides

Kim Seokjin, better known as Jin, was born on December 4, 1992, and began his journey with BTS in 2013. As one of the group's vocalists, he is recognised for his clear vocal tone, emotional delivery in ballads, and ability to bring cheerful energy to the stage.

Jin also has a distinctive identity as an entertainer. His spontaneity, humour, and interactions with fans have made him especially close to ARMY. Yet behind his entertaining personality, his solo musical journey reveals discipline, a willingness to explore new genres, and a determination to keep growing as an artist.

SOLO MILESTONES

From "The Astronaut" to "ECHO"

The Astronaut (2022)

Jin's collaboration with Coldplay marked an important milestone in his solo career. The song showcased his emotional vocals and explored themes of love, farewell, and his connection with fans. It also became a significant solo release before Jin began his mandatory military service.

Happy (2024)

After completing his military service in June 2024, Jin returned with his first solo EP, Happy, in November 2024. The project features pop-rock sounds and conveys messages about happiness, hope, and finding joy in everyday life.

Echo (May 16, 2025)

His second EP, Echo, features seven tracks and is built around pop and rock sounds with band music influences. Jin explores how everyday experiences and emotions can resonate throughout a person's life. The title track, Don't Say You Love Me, tells the story of a relationship that is falling apart but remains difficult to let go of.

The album reached No. 1 on iTunes album charts in 63 countries, while its title track topped iTunes song charts in 61 countries. Echo also debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200, marking Jin's highest solo album position at that time.

RUNSEOKJIN

A Solo Tour That Made History

In 2025, Jin embarked on his first solo tour, #RUNSEOKJIN_EP.TOUR, celebrating his albums Happy and Echo. The tour comprised 18 performances across nine cities in South Korea, Japan, the United States, and Europe.

Among the reported achievements, Jin became the first Korean solo singer to perform at The O2 Arena in London. The tour also attracted large audiences at Honda Center in Anaheim, while the performance at American Airlines Center in Dallas was reported as sold out.

The Solo Tour Finale

The main tour concluded at Ziggo Dome in Amsterdam on August 10, 2025. Jin later returned for the special #RUNSEOKJIN_EP.TOUR_ENCORE concerts at Incheon Munhak Main Stadium on October 31 and November 1, 2025. The encore brought his first solo tour journey to a special close, featuring memorable performances and appearances by fellow BTS members as special guests.

THE LATEST CHAPTER

Reunited with BTS — ARIRANG 2026

Gwanghwamun Square · March 21, 2026

Jin returned to the stage alongside the other six BTS members for a full-group performance after nearly four years. BTS THE COMEBACK LIVE | ARIRANG at Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul became a significant moment for the group and fans around the world.

Netflix reported that the livestream attracted approximately 18.4 million global views (Live+1), entered the weekly Top 10 in 80 countries, and reached No. 1 in 24 countries.

This was more than just a comeback — it marked the beginning of a new chapter for BTS as all seven members returned to creating music and performing together.

BEYOND THE STAGE

Humanitarian Contributions and Values

Jin's influence extends beyond music. He has also been associated with charitable initiatives and the positive messages BTS shares with communities around the world.

Personal Support for UNICEF Korea

In May 2019, UNICEF Korea confirmed that Jin's personal donations since May 2018 had exceeded 100 million won. Through this contribution, he became a member of the UNICEF Honors Club, a group of donors who contribute at least 100 million won. His donations had initially been made privately before being revealed, with the hope of inspiring others to contribute as well.

LOVE MYSELF — A Joint Initiative with BTS

Jin is also part of BTS and BIGHIT MUSIC's collective initiative with UNICEF through the LOVE MYSELF campaign. The campaign supports efforts to protect children and young people from violence while raising awareness of mental health through the #OnMyMind initiative. According to UNICEF, by March 2024 the campaign had contributed more than 8.9 billion won to related programmes.

Important: The LOVE MYSELF figure represents collective contributions from BTS, BIGHIT MUSIC, and fan support — not Jin's individual donations alone.
